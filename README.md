## My Favorite Movie: *Charlie and the Chocolate Factory* (2005) 🍫🎫

![Director](https://img.shields.io/badge/director-Tim%20Burton-purple) ![Year](https://img.shields.io/badge/released-2005-brown) ![Runtime](https://img.shields.io/badge/runtime-115%20min-blue)

> A poor but kind boy finds one of five Golden Tickets and wins a tour of the most amazing chocolate factory in the world.

**Charlie and the Chocolate Factory** is a fantasy movie directed by *Tim Burton*. It is based on the **1964 novel by Roald Dahl** and was released in the US on **July 15, 2005** by Warner Bros. Pictures.

### Quick Facts

### Main Characters

1. **Willy Wonka** – the owner of the chocolate factory (*Johnny Depp*)
2. **Charlie Bucket** – the kind boy who finds the last Golden Ticket (*Freddie Highmore*)
3. **Grandpa Joe** – Charlie's grandfather who joins him on the tour (*David Kelly*)
4. **The Oompa-Loompas** – the factory workers (*Deep Roy*)

### The Other Golden Ticket Winners

- Augustus Gloop
- Veruca Salt
- Violet Beauregarde
- Mike Teavee
  - Each of them gets in trouble because of a bad habit

### Why I Like It

- I am so amused by the concept of an **edible factory**
  - Almost everything in the Chocolate Room can be eaten
  - Every room is colorful and creative
- I love **Willy Wonka's personality**
  - He is weird, funny, and unpredictable
- ~~Just a candy movie~~ A movie with a real lesson about being good
